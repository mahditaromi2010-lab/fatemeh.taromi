About

I am a mechanical engineering graduate (BSc, Islamic Azad University Bandar Abbas, 2025, GPA 16.91/20) with two years of hands-on experience in a building engineering design office. My day-to-day work is designing gas piping systems for residential and commercial buildings, drawing them in AutoCAD and writing the calculation reports that go to the building authority for approval.

I hold a 390-hour gas pipe fitting skills certificate (score 84/100) and a business licence for gas pipe fitting and appliance installation, so I understand both the drawing board and the site. I have completed training in mechanical services design, fire suppression equipment and off-grid solar systems, and I work in Autodesk Inventor as well as AutoCAD. SolidWorks and Revit are on my learning list; the concepts transfer and I pick up new CAD tools quickly.
